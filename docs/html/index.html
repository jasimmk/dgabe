01 / THE CALL

Somebody has to
keep the lights growing.

Above the clouds, Gabe tends a chain of floating greenhouses. Then a storm gets into the machinery.

The arches go dark. The tenders turn hostile. A fault travels inward from the outer stormworks, and the youngest plants are running out of night.

Gabe takes his pulse sprayer, straps on a capacitor pack and begins his rounds. Find each garden's sunseed. Carry it to the exit arch. Follow the damage all the way to its source.

02 / THE EXPEDITION

THE WORK DOESN'T END AT THE SIXTH ARCH

Twelve gardens.
One way toward dawn.

Start with your boots on solid ground. End among roof seeds, guarded refills and gaps that demand every last spark. Open a garden to read its story and route.

01—06 / INNER GARDENS 07—12 / OUTER STORMWORKS
01INNER GARDENSRainwake Nursery+

Gabe starts where he usually begins his morning rounds: beside the youngest plants. Getting this little nursery breathing again gives him a reason to believe the rest of the gardens can survive the night.

Your challenge

The nursery teaches Gabe to trust his boots before his backpack. Hop onto the low planting platforms, collect the pulse sprayer near the entrance and jump over the two-tile electrical storm bed. The sunseed rests on a raised platform farther right, with a crawler on that same ledge. Clear its patrol before committing to the landing; a second crawler guards the approach to the arch. There is no flight refill here: movement, jumping and a well-aimed pulse are your starting tools.

02INNER GARDENSCopper Canopy+

Gabe recognizes the copperwork overhead; he has patched those pipes through more rainy seasons than he can count. Tonight, the machines he once tended block his way. Saving the canopy means finding a path through his own workshop.

Your challenge

The copper walkways carry two separate two-tile storm beds, with a crawler between them and a sentry farther along the route. The sunseed sits higher than in the nursery, above a pair of raised planting platforms. A second crawler patrols beside that upper sunseed, making the last jump a guarded landing. Climb by jumping between platforms, then plan your descent around the electrical beds and the sentry's horizontal shots. This garden also has no flight refill.

03INNER GARDENSCloudroot Lift+

Roots hang over the place where a walkway used to be. Gabe cannot mend the missing span tonight, but the garden on its far side still needs him. For the first time, he must trust the charge on his back as much as the ground below.

Your challenge

Here the floor breaks away into an eight-tile gap that requires flight. Collect the capacitor cell, then clear a two-tile storm bed and an approach crawler before committing to the crossing. The sunseed waits on a raised platform beyond the gap, with another cell nearby and a crawler guarding its landing platform. A sentry threatens the route below and another crawler patrols closer to the exit: four machines in all. Each cell provides seven seconds of flight. Release flight when you can safely land; holding it down spends the reserve even when you do not need more lift.

04INNER GARDENSGlasswind Gallery+

The gallery once caught the first light above the clouds. Now its highest sunseed is the brightest thing behind the glass. Gabe follows that small light upward, carrying the hope of the lower gardens into the rafters.

Your challenge

The sunseed shines near the roof, above a staircase of planting platforms. First climb to the capacitor cell on the trellis; flight must be earned by reaching it. Its charge lasts about 4.3 seconds, less than in Cloudroot Lift. A crawler patrols the middle trellis before an upper sentry threatens the high seed approach, adding close combat to the climb. Another crawler below, a three-tile storm bed and a sentry near the exit make the descent dangerous too. Four machines cover the floor, middle ledge and upper route.

05INNER GARDENSThunder Orchard+

The orchard is the part of the garden Gabe most wants to see in daylight again. Its broken skybridge makes the distance feel enormous. He checks his charge, looks toward the planting beds beyond the gap and commits to the crossing.

Your challenge

An early sentry, two two-tile storm beds and an approach crawler lead toward a nine-tile flight gap. Each cell now gives only 2.5 seconds of flight, so use lift carefully and coast into the landing rather than holding it throughout the journey. The sunseed rests on the raised platform beyond the gap, now guarded by an upper landing patrol, with another cell on the lower route there. A sentry, a second four-tile gap and a landing crawler still separate Gabe from the arch. Five machines and two crossings demand a plan for the return to ground; use a jump for the smaller gap and conserve charge for when you need it.

06INNER GARDENSEye of the Garden+

Five arches are awake behind him. At the Eye of the Garden, Gabe expects the weather to ease. Instead the restored glass catches a line of light running beyond the inner greenhouses. The damage has a source, and the garden's own outer machinery is carrying it inward. Saving his home now means following that line.

Your challenge

This midpoint combines the inner gardens' lessons with cells lasting about 1.8 seconds each. An early cell leads into a three-tile storm bed and two close crawler patrols. The next cell sits between those ground threats and an approach sentry before a nine-tile flight gap. Land and climb to the elevated refill before taking the two-tier route to the sunseed. Two elevated sentries threaten that ascent, and a five-tile crossing leads toward a crawler and the arch. Six machines, two gaps and staged refills prepare Gabe for the outer stormworks.

07OUTER STORMWORKSAshglass Foundry+

The foundry once shaped the panes that kept the nursery warm. Its dark glass now reflects six small lights behind Gabe, each one a garden he has brought back. He follows the copperwork through the old workshop, carrying a living sunseed where heat and machinery used to do the tending. Restoring this arch is his first step beyond the home he knows.

Your challenge

A low canopy covers the approach to the first eight-tile gap. Reach the landing trellis for a refill, then climb to another cell and the elevated sunseed while sentries guard the trays. A seven-tile gap, a storm bed and exit-side machines still lie ahead. Each cell supplies 100 steps, about 1.67 seconds, so make each landing part of the route.

08OUTER STORMWORKSLightning Aqueduct+

The aqueduct carried water through the floating gardens long before Gabe learned their paths. Tonight its glass channels hold the storm's reflection instead of the dawn. Following them outward, he begins to understand how a fault in one place can travel through every place that depends on it. Another sunseed, another arch: keep the line alive without letting its damage follow him home.

Your challenge

Cross three missing spans of seven, seven and six tiles. The landing trellises carry cells and shooting sentries, making each refill a position to earn. The sunseed waits on the final raised tray, with more opposition between it and the arch. Cells still supply 100 steps each; aim for a controlled landing before the next crossing.

09OUTER STORMWORKSBlackthorn Relay+

Blackthorn has grown around the relay's copper ribs. Gabe remembers pruning it when the tenders still knew his footsteps. The relay links the inner gardens to the outer works; its silence explains why the warning never reached the nursery. He threads a living light through the place that should have carried the message, tending the connection one arch at a time.

Your challenge

The first eight-tile crossing leads to an ascending route of trellises, elevated refills and a high sunseed. Sentries cover the climb while a crawler patrols below. After collecting the seed, descend toward a ground refill before the seven- and six-tile crossings. Cells now supply 95 steps, about 1.58 seconds, so height and safe landing space matter together.

10OUTER STORMWORKSCyclone Conservatory+

The conservatory was built for the plants that needed the open sky. Gabe finds them bowed beneath a sky that will not stop moving. Eleven kinds of leaf once grew here, each bending differently in a gentle wind; tonight they all point toward the same dark center. He brings the next sunseed forward because a garden built to meet the weather must also be given a chance to survive it.

Your challenge

Climb through three elevated tiers to the roof-level cell and sunseed after the first eight-tile crossing. Sentries guard upper lanes, while the later eight- and seven-tile gaps require a descent to staged landing refills. Keep the 95-step cell limit in mind before leaving an upper platform; clear the next lane and commit to a reachable landing.

11OUTER STORMWORKSStarfall Engine+

In the engine house, the storm sounds almost regular. Gabe hears the same broken rhythm that reached the nursery beneath the rain. This is the machinery that should have balanced the outer works, carrying the fault through the network instead. The seed in his hands is small beside it, but he has learned that a place does not need a grand rescue to begin working again.

Your challenge

Four crossings of seven, eight, seven and six tiles alternate with elevated cells and sentry-guarded trays. A higher refill interrupts the route between the middle spans, and the sunseed rests beyond that climb. Cells give only 90 steps, or 1.5 seconds: trade stored height for distance, fire before crossing into a guarded lane and reach the next refill before spending more charge.

12OUTER STORMWORKSDawnseed Crown+

At the crown, the sky begins to pale. The restored inner gardens are only points of light below Gabe, but together they trace every step that brought him here. The last sunseed belongs in the crown's exit arch, where the outer network can answer the gardens instead of feeding the storm. He carries it down from the roof and across the remaining spans as a gardener finishing his rounds: careful, tired and unwilling to leave one living place behind.

Your challenge

This is the campaign's true finale. Four crossings of eight, eight, eight and seven tiles surround a climb to the roof seed, with elevated cells and sentries along the ascent. The 85-step cells provide about 1.42 seconds of thrust each. Prepare the approach, land for each staged refill and save enough height to carry the dawnseed across the remaining spans. The 84-tile route ends with a solid apron leading to the arch, guarded by the final sentry. Eleven machines include eight sentries, increasing ranged pressure beyond the previous stage.

Build tools & release notes

Use CMake, a C/C++ compiler and Rust. Windows uses the MSVC toolchain; macOS uses Xcode Command Line Tools. Linux additionally needs its display and audio development libraries. The repository guide lists the exact packages and commands.

Apple mobile builds use Xcode, an installed iOS SDK and the project's iOS build script. Physical-device installation requires appropriate signing and provisioning. An unsigned device ZIP is a build artifact, not an installable app. A simulator ZIP runs only in a compatible simulator.

The release workflow packages desktop archives, a universal iPhone/iPad unsigned build and an arm64 simulator build. A signed IPA is optional when valid Apple signing configuration is supplied. GitHub artifacts are not App Store or TestFlight publication.

All controls & the four-life rule

Hold pulse after finding the sprayer; hold flight while charge remains. Both Control and Shift keys work. Jump is a press while grounded.

Enter or Space begins from the title, resumes a pause, advances after a cleared stage, or starts a new campaign after victory or defeat. P / Esc pauses or resumes play; it does not quit. R retries the current garden while playing or paused. Q or closing the window quits.

Four lives carry through the campaign. Damage spends a life and restarts the garden; manual retry costs no life. A restart rolls back the stage's score, pickups and tools. Each new stage starts with fresh tools and charge. Touch controls offer movement, flight, pulse, jump, pause and retry.
